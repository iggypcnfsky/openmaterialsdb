# Data licence

The curated dataset in this folder (`materials/*.json`, `sources.json`) is dedicated to the public domain under
**CC0 1.0 Universal** (https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/), as decided in the project's decision log.

Each value cites its origin in `metadata.sources`. Third-party sources keep their own terms:
- Physically Based (physicallybased.info) — CC0 1.0.
- Wikipedia — CC BY-SA 4.0 (only individual facts are cited, no text is copied).
- MakeItFrom.com, The Wood Database, Toray, Diab, Easy Composites — facts cited with links; no pages or price lists are reproduced.

`raw/sheet_lista_materialow.csv` is an export of the project planning sheet; its numbers are **unverified** and are kept only under
`sheet_indicative`. `raw/pb_materials.json` is a snapshot of the Physically Based API (CC0).
